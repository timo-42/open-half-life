# ohl_campaign::STARTMAP ("c0a0") lands in, walked from its arrival point.

# Project-authored; no bytes here come from any game installation (see
# docs/CLEAN_ROOM.md). Named by its position in the chain rather than by
# the map it runs on, for the reason recorded in c0a0-hop1.txt and in
# xtask/src/chain_walk.rs's module documentation.
#
# A line's leading number is a count of *ticks*, each one CAPTURE_STEP
# (1/60 s) of simulated time — not seconds.
#
# Route, in words only (no entity name, path or coordinate from any
# payload): the ride the previous two routes were on is still under the
# player when this one starts, and it is still the whole route. The player
# presses nothing. The car runs its track out shortly after arriving, is
# carried down and round onto the next one by the moving piece of track
# waiting at that dead end, rides on through the doors that section of the
# ride opens ahead of itself, pauses where the ride is scripted to pause,
# and finishes at a level change the end of the ride fires by name — which
# is where `run_chained` ends the route.
#
# M9.18 could not do any of that. Two published `path_track`/track-change
# keyvalues were recorded as documented-but-unimplemented, so the car
# simply stopped at the dead end: the ride's remaining nodes were never
# passed, the two `func_door`s the reachability walk found closed and
# not use-openable from anywhere reachable were never fired open (the
# only thing in that map that names them is a node the car never reached),
# and this route had to step the player off the parked car into open
# ground that leads nowhere. M9.19 implements both keyvalues and the route
# is the ride again; see docs/MILESTONES.md M9.19 and
# docs/FORMAT_SOURCES.md, "Track trains and paths".
#
# The trailing wait is slack: `run_chained` (crates/ohl-app/src/game_run.rs)
# ends a route at the level change it reaches, so any ticks left over here
# are never run.
